# Orga

Orga runs routines of activities on your Mac and tells you when to switch: for example, work for 40 minutes, walk 100 steps, and repeat until you stop. It keeps a log of what you did, so you can see where your time went.

**[Download Orga](https://github.com/SergioFelperin/orga-app/releases/latest/download/Orga.zip)** · [All versions and what's new](https://github.com/SergioFelperin/orga-app/releases) · [User manual](MANUAL.md)

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Install

1. Download **Orga.zip** with the link above and double-click it to unzip.
2. Drag **Orga** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open Orga. Because it isn't distributed through the App Store, macOS blocks it the first time:
    1. When macOS says it can't verify Orga, click **Done**.
    2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Orga, and click **Open Anyway**.
    3. Confirm with your password, then click **Open**.
4. When Orga asks to send notifications, click **Allow**.

You only need step 3 once per version.

## Update

Download the new version, quit Orga, and replace the copy in Applications. Your programs and history are kept.

## Uninstall

Quit Orga and move it from Applications to the Trash. To also remove your programs and history, delete the folder `~/Library/Application Support/Orga` (in the Finder, choose **Go → Go to Folder…** and paste that path).

## Privacy

Orga works entirely on your Mac. It doesn't connect to the internet, and your programs and history never leave your computer.

## Help

The [user manual](MANUAL.md) is also inside the app: choose **Help → Orga Help**. To report a problem or suggest something, [open an issue](https://github.com/SergioFelperin/orga-app/issues).
